Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how Underclipped Uploader ("the tool") handles data. The tool is used only by the owner of the Underclipped YouTube channel.

Use of YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the Underclipped channel. By using the tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

  • An OAuth access token and refresh token for the Underclipped YouTube channel, with the youtube.upload permission only.
  • The video files, titles and descriptions that the tool itself creates and uploads.

The tool does not read, collect or store data about other YouTube users, viewers, comments or analytics.

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • The tokens are used only to upload videos to the Underclipped channel.
  • The tokens are stored on a private, password-protected storage device owned by the channel owner, on a home network. They are never sold or shared with third parties.
  • No cookies or tracking are used on this website or in the tool.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings page. After revocation, or on request, the stored tokens are deleted within 7 days.
